# 《稳稳接住》Show Notes style

Use this guide only after the final cut and final timestamped transcript are confirmed.

## Voice

- Enter through a concrete scene, phrase, public event, or moment from the episode.
- Name a feeling that was previously difficult to describe.
- Move from the external event into a specific inner conflict and lived experience.
- Preserve the hosts' natural breathing room. Avoid symmetrical AI-sounding slogans.
- Keep facts, stories, and quotations traceable to the final-cut transcript.

## Hard language checks

- Do not use em-dash punctuation in prose.
- Do not use the Chinese contrast construction matching `不是.{0,40}而是`.
- Do not invent opposition merely to manufacture a quotable line.
- Do not open with generic phrases such as “本期我们探讨了” or “希望给你带来启发”.

## Structure

### Title

Use: episode number + memorable phrase, concept, or scene + one concrete human question.

### Opening

Write three or four short paragraphs:

1. A concrete trigger from the final episode.
2. The feeling or discomfort it names.
3. The path the conversation takes through stories, conflict, and change.
4. Optional final destination of the episode, without a generic moral.

### Highlights

Select 10 to 16 by default. Each item must contain at least one of: a scene, exact quotation, conflict, emotional change, viewpoint turn, or host-to-host connection. Do not turn the section into a list of abstract concepts.

### Timeline

Select 10 to 16 final-cut timestamps. Each label should show what happens and why it matters. Avoid topic-only labels such as “关于成长”.

### Listener interaction

Ask one specific question that invites a lived experience. Do not turn it into a multi-part questionnaire.

### Fixed footer

Use the following text exactly:

哈喽哈喽，欢迎来到《稳稳接住》。这是一档由涓妹和珏妹主持的自我成长播客。

珏妹常常让谈话长出枝桠，涓妹会让感受扎下根。当然，有时候我们也会交换位置。

我们在发散与停留、碰撞与陪伴之间，试着把生活里的那些卡住、拧巴和迷茫，一点一点说开。

希望我们的对话也能稳稳接住你。让你发现，原来你不是一个人，原来生活还有另一种理解方式，那些曾经觉得过不去的时刻，也许都会慢慢过去。

也欢迎关注我们的同名小红书账号「稳稳接住」。我们会在那里分享节目切片、文字内容，以及录音之外没有讲完的话。

## Required final sections

1. Single-episode title
2. Opening paragraphs
3. `✨ 高光内容`
4. `⏱ 时间轴`
5. `💬 本期互动`
6. `🎙 关于《稳稳接住》`
